Your configuration vs. the shipped profiles

Two ready-made variable sets live in azure/profiles/. The module defaults sit between them.

Can you tear it down at will? Yes — terraform destroy per module, in reverse order. Two things to know: AI Search and API Management bill while they exist, idle or not, so destroy those first if you just want the meter to stop. And Key Vault purge protection keeps a soft-deleted vault — and its name — for 90 days with no force-purge, which is why the sandbox profile turns it off.

List prices, USD, pay-as-you-go, East US 2, no EA/CSP discount, early-2026 rates. Estimates only — confirm against the Azure Pricing Calculator before you commit budget. Power Platform and Copilot Studio are licensing and tenant capacity, not Azure metering, and are excluded from the Azure figure above unless you switch them on.
