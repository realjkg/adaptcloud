How it works

  1. Submit the fields at section 2, before substantive joint activity on the opportunity.
  2. Decision within five business days: accepted, rejected with a stated ground, or duplicate. Silence is acceptance.
  3. Acceptance creates a tracker record and starts the 90-day protection period.
  4. Keep it current. One recorded activity every 30 days holds protection open.

Register early, not when the deal looks certain. Conflicts are decided on the tracker record, so an opportunity worked but never registered has no protection to assert. Registration costs nothing and is reversible.

Decision

AdaptCloud returns one of three outcomes within five business days.

OutcomeWhenEffect
AcceptedNo conflicting prior registration and no documented prior direct pursuitTracker record created; protection period starts
RejectedPrior accepted registration for the same account and use case, documented prior direct pursuit, or a materially incomplete submissionGround stated in writing. Partner may resubmit if the ground is incompleteness
DuplicateSame account and use case already registered by another partnerFirst accepted registration prevails. A different use case at the same account may be registered separately
4

Tracker record

The tracker is the system of record and the primary evidence in any channel conflict. AdaptCloud maintains it; the Partner may request its own records at any time.

5

Status vocabulary

A registration holds exactly one status. The terms are closed; no other value is recorded.

  • SubmittedReceived, decision pending. Protection has not started.
  • AcceptedProtection running. Activity expected every 30 days.
  • RejectedNot registered. Ground recorded.
  • NoticeInactivity notice issued, cure period running.
  • LapsedCure period expired without activity. Account returned to open status.
  • WonContracted. Renewal treatment applies while the Partner remains active.
  • LostClient selected another path. Closed.
  • WithdrawnPartner withdrew. Closed immediately, no cure period.
  • ExpiredProtection period ended without extension.

Lapsed and expired are different, and both require notice. Lapsed follows inactivity and a cure period that ran out. Expired follows the clock running down while the Partner was still active but did not request an extension. Neither is applied silently.
