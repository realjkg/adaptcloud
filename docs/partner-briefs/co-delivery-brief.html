Executive summary

  1. The regulatory obligation persists notwithstanding the programme suspension. Phase II of CMMC was suspended on 13 July 2026.1 DFARS 252.204-7012, Phase I self-assessment, and submission of a NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 score to the Supplier Performance Risk System remain in force.2 Demand has shifted from certification readiness to recurring evidence maintenance.
  2. Competing propositions were constructed around a deadline that no longer exists. Vendors positioned on a November 2026 assessment date have lost their primary close. An offering priced for continuous evidence rather than a one-time sprint faces materially reduced competitive pressure in the near term.
  3. The constraint on partner margin is delivery repeatability, not demand. Readiness engagements are presently hand-constructed by senior practitioners, so unit cost does not decline across engagements. AdaptCloud supplies a versioned control library, host scanner, remediation playbooks and governed cloud landing-zone modules intended to reduce marginal delivery cost.
  4. Automated coverage is partial and is stated rather than implied. Seventeen practices across six of fourteen domains are automated at the date of issue (Exhibit 2). The residual practices are predominantly policy and process controls that are not susceptible to automated verification by any vendor.
  5. The proposed first step is a bounded 60-day pilot on one mutual client, assessed against a single quantitative exit criterion defined at section 8.
2

Market context

The Department suspended Phase II of the CMMC programme on 13 July 2026, deferring the third-party assessment requirement that had been scheduled to take effect on 10 November 2026, and constituted a reform task force to report subsequently.1 Phase I self-assessment obligations, DFARS 252.204-7012, and NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 were unaffected.2

Two consequences follow for the defence supplier base. First, the compliance obligation continues to attach to contract award, and a supplier's SPRS score degrades as its estate drifts from its assessed state, which establishes a recurring rather than one-time requirement. Second, procurement urgency has diminished, which lengthens sales cycles for propositions dependent on deadline pressure and favours propositions positioned on cost of ongoing compliance.

Published estimates of the cost of compliance are set out at Exhibit 1. They are drawn from secondary sources and have not been independently validated; they are presented to establish order of magnitude for the pricing position at section 6, not as a basis for a business case.

3

Solution description

The proposition comprises three separable components: intellectual property, an integrated solution, and a delivery construct. The partner retains the client relationship and provides the delivery organisation; AdaptCloud provides the engine, the enablement materials and second-line support.

The landing-zone component addresses governance of AI agent identities within a policy-enforced environment. This is assessed as the least substitutable element of the proposition, on the basis that it addresses a control requirement the client base is beginning to encounter and for which established delivery patterns are limited.

4

Assessed automated coverage

Coverage is published rather than characterised. Overstating automated scope is assessed as the principal reputational risk in this category, on the basis that the misstatement is discovered during assessment rather than during the sale.

4.1 Provenance classification

The evidence pipeline applies generative AI to narrative drafting. A generated narrative statement and an observed system fact are visually indistinguishable in a finished package, and conflation of the two is assessed as a material risk to the defensibility of any AI-assisted submission. The two are accordingly separated in the data model and cannot be merged in output.

  • ObservedMachine-observed. The scanner read live configuration. Carries host, timestamp, and the identifier of the check that produced it.
  • AssertedModel-asserted. Drafted from context by the AI layer. Reviewable, and not presented as an observation at any point.
  • AttestedHuman-attested. Signed by a named individual. Required before any model-asserted item enters a submitted package.
5

Data boundary and regulatory scope

The scanner executes on the client host and returns control status, findings and scores. Configuration artefacts, system files and controlled unclassified information remain within the client environment. This is an architectural constraint adopted in advance of first contract rather than a present limitation to be remediated.

The purpose of the constraint is to keep both parties outside the obligations that attach to a cloud service storing or processing CUI under DFARS 252.204-7012, including the requirement for FedRAMP Moderate authorisation or documented equivalency. Where a client requires a fully hosted posture, that constitutes a separate offering with its own authorisation path and commercial treatment, and is out of scope of this document.

6

Commercial construct

Pricing is metered on assessment scope, expressed as hosts and enclaves, and not on user seats. Scope is the unit in which the compliance buyer already reasons, and seat-based metering is assessed as adverse to penetration of the smaller subcontractor segment that primes require brought to standard.

Against the cost base at Exhibit 1, the annual subscription tiers represent under one per cent of a first compliance cycle. The recommended anchor in a client conversation is the practitioner hours displaced from assessment preparation, rather than the list price of a comparator tool.

Recommended next step

A bounded pilot on a single mutual client, over 60 days, structured in four workstreams and assessed against one quantitative exit criterion.

  • W1Selection. One mutual client with an active 800-171 obligation and an estate below 25 hosts. Partner-led.
  • W2Baseline. Partner practitioners operate the engine with AdaptCloud in support. Output is a scored, provenance-classified evidence bundle.
  • W3Remediation and re-verification of the automated control set, followed by practitioner delivery of the remaining domains on the timeline the technical evidence permits.
  • W4Validation of the Exhibit 1 cost assumptions against observed engagement effort.

Exit criterion. The proportion of delivered content originating from existing library components rather than constructed for the engagement, measured at pilot close and compared against a target agreed at initiation. This ratio determines whether the construct scales for the partner, and it is proposed as the sole basis on which the pilot is judged.
