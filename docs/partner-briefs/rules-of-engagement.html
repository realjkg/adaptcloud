1

Purpose and precedence

This addendum states how opportunities are registered, what protection registration confers, and how conflict is resolved. It exists so that both parties can act without asking permission in the ordinary case.

Where this addendum conflicts with the Partner Agreement, the Partner Agreement governs. Section 10 lists every commercial parameter in one place; those values are the negotiable part of this document.

2

Definitions

  • 2.1Partner-sourced. An opportunity the Partner identified and introduced, where AdaptCloud held no active pursuit of the same account and use case at the time of submission.
  • 2.2Registered opportunity. A partner-sourced opportunity that AdaptCloud has accepted under section 3.
  • 2.3Registered use case. The specific workload, business unit, or compliance scope described at registration. Protection attaches to the account and the use case, not to the account alone.
  • 2.4Protection period. The interval during which section 4 applies, beginning at acceptance.
  • 2.5Active pursuit. Documented partner activity on the opportunity: a client meeting, a submitted proposal, a scoping session, or a written client response, recorded in the tracker.
3

Registration

  1. The Partner submits the registration form before substantive joint activity on the opportunity.
  2. AdaptCloud responds within the decision window at section 10, with one of: accepted, rejected, or duplicate.
  3. Acceptance starts the protection period. Silence past the decision window is treated as acceptance.

3.1 Grounds for rejection

An opportunity may be rejected only where AdaptCloud holds a prior accepted registration from another partner for the same account and use case, holds a documented direct pursuit predating the submission, or the submission is materially incomplete. Rejection states the ground.

3.2 Duplicate submissions

First accepted registration prevails. A second partner registering the same account for a different use case may be accepted, and both registrations then stand, each protected within its own scope.

4

Account protection

A partner-sourced opportunity becomes protected upon accepted deal registration. During the protection period, AdaptCloud will not pursue, quote, contract with, or solicit the registered account for the registered use case except through the originating partner, absent written consent.

Protection binds AdaptCloud's direct sales activity. It does not restrict the client from approaching AdaptCloud, and it does not restrict AdaptCloud from serving the same account through a different partner for a different, unregistered use case.

Where an inbound client request touches a registered scope, AdaptCloud refers the client to the originating partner and notifies the Partner within two business days.

5

Activity and lapse

Protection is conditional on pursuit. Where no active pursuit is recorded within the inactivity window at section 10, AdaptCloud issues written notice and the Partner has the cure period to record activity or request an extension.

If neither occurs, the registration lapses and the account returns to open status. Lapse is never automatic and never silent: it requires notice and an unanswered cure period.

The Partner may request one extension of the protection period, granted where active pursuit is evidenced.

6

Renewal and expansion

Where the Partner remains active on the account, the Partner holds first right on renewal of the registered subscription, and renewal compensation follows the margin framework in the partner economics schedule.

Expansion into a new use case at the same account requires a new registration. It is not covered by the original.

7

Pricing governance

AdaptCloud will not quote below the agreed partner price floor into a registered account for the registered use case. Any exception requires written approval from both parties before the quote is issued.

Where the client solicits pricing directly, AdaptCloud provides list pricing only and refers the client to the Partner.

8

Conflict resolution

  1. Raise in writing to the named channel contacts on both sides, with the tracker reference.
  2. Joint review within the conflict window at section 10. The tracker record is the primary evidence.
  3. Decision and record. The outcome is written into the tracker, including any agreed remedy.
  4. Escalation to named leadership on both sides if unresolved at step 3.

Conflicts are decided on the tracker record. An opportunity worked but never registered has no protection to assert. Register early.

9

Term and survival

This addendum runs with the Partner Agreement. On termination, registrations accepted and still active continue to their scheduled expiry, and the non-circumvention obligation at section 4 survives for the period stated at section 10 in respect of those accounts.

Nothing here restricts either party from serving accounts that were never registered.
