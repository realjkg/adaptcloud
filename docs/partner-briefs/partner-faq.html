Partner questions, answered directly

The questions partners ask, with the mechanism behind each answer. Where the answer is a limit, we say so.

Working with a newer vendor

Why bet a client relationship on a new vendor?

Do not bet the account on us. Start with a bounded, partner-led proof engagement with a defined scope, written acceptance criteria, and a joint escalation model. If it does not meet the criteria, it ends there and the client relationship is untouched.

MechanismPaid pilot SOWReadiness checklistAcceptance testNamed escalation pathPost-engagement report

Can a team this size support us?

We cap accredited launch cohorts and delivery capacity, and we do not oversell. The model transfers repeatable work to trained partner teams while AdaptCloud retains product escalations. If we cannot support a cohort, we say so before you commit sellers, not after.

MechanismCapacity planPartner tieringOnboarding gatesService-level boundaries

Who are your references?

We will not invent references. For initial programs we offer a joint reference path: an NDA case study with a measured baseline and outcome, and the option for the partner to use the engagement as its own reference. Reference rights move up a ladder — internal evidence, anonymized case study, named case study, reference call — each step requiring specific client permission.

MechanismReference agreementAnonymized case-study templateClient permission workflow

What if you do not survive?

We address continuity contractually and architecturally: exportable evidence in open formats, documented configurations, and an agreed transition or escrow plan proportionate to the engagement. Your client's evidence is not hostage to our corporate status.

Delivery and operational risk

What if it fails in a client environment?

The partner remains the client-facing delivery lead. AdaptCloud provides second-line product support, documented rollback procedures, and agreed severity targets for the supported scope. Ownership of the client conversation does not change mid-incident.

MechanismSupport policyIncident processRelease notesRollback planSeverity matrix

Can our consultants deliver it without you?

Yes, after accreditation. We co-deliver the first engagements to transfer the method; accredited consultants lead subsequent engagements while we provide second-line support. Only accredited personnel may lead a production delivery, which is a rule we hold to rather than a preference.

Will this work for our clients' environments?

We qualify environment fit before the statement of work. Where prerequisites are not met we identify that explicitly rather than burying it in implementation and discovering it in week three.

What is the implementation timeline?

We provide a phased plan with prerequisites, partner and client responsibilities, weekly checkpoints, and a written definition of done. We do not quote a universal timeline before qualification, because a timeline given before discovery is a guess wearing a date.

Commercial terms

Where is the margin?

We publish margin bands by role and attach model. Partners choose the commercial model per deal, and the economics are transparent before you invest sales or delivery capacity. Bands rather than one fixed percentage, because deal quality, services intensity, and volume all move the number.

MechanismRate cardDiscount scheduleSample deal economicsProgram funds where offered

Will you go direct on our demand?

No. Registered accounts are partner-protected. We do not sell direct into partner-sourced or partner-led registered opportunities without written consent. It is a contract term, not an assurance.

MechanismDeal registrationProtection termNon-circumvention clauseCRM attribution

Will you undercut us on price?

Partner-led opportunities follow registered-opportunity pricing. Any exception requires joint approval, and we do not quote below the agreed partner floor into protected accounts.

Can we white-label it?

We support a defined co-branded or partner-led delivery model. White-labeling is a commercial and support commitment rather than an informal promise, and it is available only at accredited tiers.

Three separable components: platform subscription, partner-delivered professional services, and optional AdaptCloud enablement and support. Customers and partners can see each cost and each responsibility rather than a bundled figure.

Product, evidence, and AI

Why not use an established platform?

Use the incumbent where it is already the operational standard. Use AdaptCloud where the constraint is repeatable evidence, faster control implementation, or a partner-owned delivery motion. We compete on a defined wedge and do not claim to replace every platform.

MechanismNarrow-use-case comparisonTime-to-evidence benchmarkLibrary coverage metrics

Is the evidence defensible?

Every output is traceable to its source, version, execution context, reviewer, and supporting artifact. We distinguish platform-generated evidence from client-attested evidence, so a reviewer can tell which is which without asking.

MechanismProvenance modelEvidence schemaSample exportAudit trailQA review procedure

Is AI creating unacceptable risk?

AI assists analysis and workflow. It does not replace accountable human review and does not make unsubstantiated compliance determinations. High-impact outputs are reviewable, traceable, and policy-controlled.
